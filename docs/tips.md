# Tips and questions

[← Back to the README](../README.md)

## Playing

- **Fly and phone calls need the open air,** and the PC is indoors: use them from WoobPad.
- **Boxes follows the game's rules:** your party keeps a Pokémon that can battle, a Pokémon holding mail
  can't go in a box (take the letter off in Mail first), and Eggs can't be released.
- **WoobPad** isn't in the START menu in a link room or the Safari Zone. Decorate stays at the PC.
- **Notifications** wait until you're free to move, and aren't kept after you close the game.

## Your save

- **You can turn WoobOS on and off whenever you like.** It keeps only its own things in your save
  (R-Bay bids, a GTS deposit, teams on errands, the Bank, this week's challenges), and they wait for it
  while it's off.
- **R-Bay bids** are saved with your game: load an older save and its bids and money come back with it.
  A won Pokémon waits if your party and PC are both full.
- **Settings** are kept for the whole game, not per save file. Markers and waypoints belong to each save.

## Making it yours

- **Your own wallpapers:** put PNG or JPG pictures in the mod's `wallpapers` folder and pick them in
  Settings. SELECT switches between filling the screen and stretching to it.
- **Your own language:** copy `lang/en.lua` to a new file in the same folder, write the language's name
  at the top and translate the words. It shows up in Settings. WoobOS comes in English, French, German,
  Spanish, Italian, Japanese and Brazilian Portuguese.
- **Your own apps:** other mods can add apps to the desktop. See [DEVELOPMENT.md](../DEVELOPMENT.md).
