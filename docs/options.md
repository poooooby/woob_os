# Options

In the game's OPTIONS menu, WOOBOS opens these directly. They're also under MODS > WoobOS.

A few of them are in WoobOS's own Settings app too (the theme, the text size and Overworld GPS).
Whichever place you changed last is the one that counts.

[← Back to the README](../README.md)

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Desktop | On / Off | On | Off: every PC opens the game's own menu. |
| Boot Screen | On / Off | On | The W boot screen before the desktop. |
| 24-Hour Clock | On / Off | Off | The clock as 15:30 instead of 3:30 PM. |
| Theme | Light / Dark / OLED | Light | The look of every WoobOS screen; OLED's backgrounds are true black. The Pokémon Center keeps its own look, and the market stalls keep their scene for the time of day. |
| Text Size | Normal / Large / Larger | Normal | Bigger text in every app. |
| WoobPad | On / Off | On | Off: no WoobPad and no notification banners over the field (the PC still shows them), and the START menu's PokéNav (Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald) and Pokégear (Gold, Silver, Crystal) are the game's own. |
| Pokégear | On / Off | On | Gold, Silver and Crystal. Off: the game's own Pokégear. |
| Real Shops | On / Off | On | Off: every shop counter opens the game's own shop menu. |
| Pokémon Center | On / Off | On | Off: the nurse's counter is the game's own. |
| Modern Menus | On / Off | On | WoobOS's look for the game's menus, each with its own switch below (shown while it's on). Off: the game's own, every one. |
| - Pokédex | On / Off | On | Off: the game's own Pokédex, and no Pokédex app. Shown while Modern Menus is on. |
| - Bag | On / Off | On | The Bag, and choosing from it (planting a Berry, the Berry Blender, the PC's items, selling, the Battle Pyramid). Off: the game's own Bag. Shown while Modern Menus is on. |
| - Pokémon Menu | On / Off | On | The Pokémon menu, and choosing from it (giving and using items, a team, the Move Tutor). Off: the game's own. Shown while Modern Menus is on. |
| - Options | On / Off | On | OPTIONS in glass: whole names, switches and arrows, the rows other mods add tagged and set apart, and each mod's options page with its options as a tree and their descriptions. Off: the game's own. Shown while Modern Menus is on. |
| - Summary | On / Off | On | A Pokémon's summary, and choosing a move to forget. Off: the game's own. Shown while Modern Menus is on. |
| - Evolution | On / Off | On | A Pokémon evolving, full screen with light and sparkles: the old one and the new one trading places, the flash, the new one. Off: the game's own. Shown while Modern Menus is on. |
| - Catch & Name | On / Off | On | After a first catch, the Pokémon's Pokédex page and the question to nickname it, with a modern keyboard; the same keyboard for every Pokémon's nickname (a catch, an Egg, a gift). Off: the game's own. Shown while Modern Menus is on. |
| - Battle Menus | On / Off | On | Off: the game's own Bag, Pokémon menu and summary in a battle (out of battle, as their own switches say). Shown while Modern Menus is on. |
| - Kanto Gear Battle Menus | On / Off | Off | With Kanto Gear showing its own screen (a second display, or beside the game), a battle's Bag, Pokémon menu and summary open there, larger, and the battle stays on the game's screen. Shown while Modern Menus is on. |
| Overworld GPS | On / Off | Off | With a waypoint set, an arrow by your trainer on the field points the way (FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, Gold, Silver, Crystal). |
| Map: Invert X | On / Off | Off | The 3D map's camera turns the other way. |
| Map: Invert Y | On / Off | Off | The 3D map's camera tilts the other way. |
