# What each game has

WoobOS works on all eleven of Gen1Recomp's games. The desktop, WoobPad and most apps are the same
everywhere, with each game's own Pokémon, items and moves. A few things depend on what the game itself
had.

[← Back to the README](../README.md)

## FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald

Everything, including the modern [Bag, Pokémon menu and summary](in-game.md).

- **Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald:** WoobPad takes the PokéNav's place in the START menu, and Condition
  and Ribbons are theirs alone. The Phone is Match Call on Emerald and Trainer's Eyes on Ruby
  and Sapphire.
- **Emerald:** the 3D map shows Kyogre's and Groudon's storms where they're rumoured.
- **The News** follows the region's story here: Team Rocket in Kanto and the Sevii Islands, or Team
  Magma and Team Aqua in Hoenn.
- **Slots'** 10th Anniversary Pokémon, specials and event tickets are on these games, each ticket on the
  games whose harbor takes it.

## Gold, Silver and Crystal

- WoobPad takes the Pokégear's place in the START menu: its Map, Phone and Radio do the Pokégear's jobs,
  and Fly and the wall maps open the 3D map.
- The **Radio** app is theirs alone.
- The **GPS** knows their whirlpools, waterfalls and story roadblocks: see [the map and GPS](map.md).

## Red, Blue and Yellow

- No Mail, Phone, Radio or shiny Pokémon, as in the games themselves.
- Their Map can't Fly and has no GPS, but markers work.

## Item pictures on the Game Boy games

Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver and Crystal have no pictures for their items. If you've also imported
Emerald, FireRed or LeafGreen, the Mart, the Bag and the other apps show that game's item pictures (a TM
as a disc in its move's type's colour). Otherwise each item is a tile in its pocket's colour.

## Not on the Game Boy games

Condition, Ribbons and the PokéNav are Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald's only.
