Your own wallpapers for WoobleOS.

Put PNG or JPG pictures in this folder, then open Settings > Wallpaper on the PC's desktop: each
picture shows up there under its file name. With one of your pictures chosen, switch between:

  Fill     the picture covers the screen, cropped from its centre (a wide one, 16:9 or so, loses
           the least)
  Stretch  the whole picture, stretched to the screen's shape

Press SELECT on the Wallpaper page, or tap Fill / Stretch by its title. Any size will do.
